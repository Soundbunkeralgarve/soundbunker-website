Finding SoundBunker Algarve × The Hub Culture

We’re located next to the iconic Loulé Municipal Market inside The Hub Culture, right in the heart of Loulé.

Parking & arrival information

Parking in the historic centre can be busy, especially on market days, so we recommend allowing an extra 5–10 minutes.

Parque de Estacionamento Municipal
Covered public car park just a short walk away. Open 24/7 and ideal if street parking is full.

Parque de Estacionamento da Moura Cássima
Another nearby public parking option within walking distance.

Parking Santa Luzia
Free parking a little further out — around a 5–10 minute walk.

Street Parking
Pay-and-display spaces are available around the market and surrounding streets, although these fill quickly during busy periods.

Bookings: bookings@soundbunker.pt · @soundbunkerpt
