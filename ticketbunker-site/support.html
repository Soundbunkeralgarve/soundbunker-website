QUICK HELP

Before you send.

I can't find my ticket.

Open My tickets using the verified email used for the booking. Check your confirmation email and junk folder. If it still isn't there, open a Tickets & admission case. Don't share your QR code publicly.

My event was cancelled or postponed.

Check My tickets and your booking notices for the options offered. Whole-event cancellations of events booked and ticketed directly with TicketBunker use our automatic cancellation-refund process. A supplier purchase may have a different seller and process, shown before payment. Contact us if something is unclear.

Can I get a refund because my plans changed?

Tickets for a specified date are normally non-refundable for a change of mind, subject to the booking terms and your legal rights. Open-date vouchers may have different rules. A cancellation, material change, invalid ticket or payment error needs its own review. See the refund policy.

Does “approved” mean the money is back?

No. A request can be received, under review, approved, processing or confirmed refunded. We show the stage reported for a linked request. Bank processing can continue after a payment-provider confirmation.

I need help with a supplier booking.

Include the supplier name and booking reference. We will explain the next step for that booking. If you paid on another provider's site, use its confirmation to find the seller's official support. For a supplier booking sold through TicketBunker, keep your case here and we will coordinate the next step. Keep QR codes and passwords private; your statutory rights remain protected.

I want to raise a complaint or question a decision.

Open a Complaint case or reply to your existing case with the reference and the outcome you are seeking. Ask for a review if you disagree with a refund decision. You can also use the contact and dispute-resolution information in our terms.
