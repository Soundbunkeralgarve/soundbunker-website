Events & activities in London

Allow time for the event

Plan backwards from doors or your activity meeting time. Leave room for delays, baggage collection and the trip from the station or airport. Check the final train or coach home if you are returning the same night.

Compare more than the fare

For car hire, check deposits and rental conditions. For rail and coach travel, check the arrival stop, changes, seat reservations and refund restrictions. Prices and routes are provided by each booking site when you search.

Plan each booking around the others

Check the event confirmation before committing to non-refundable travel. When travelling as a group, agree the departure point and arrival window before everyone books their own journey.
