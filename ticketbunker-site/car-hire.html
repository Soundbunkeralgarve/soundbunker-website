Get the pickup details right

Check the exact collection location, desk hours and return time. An airport desk and an off-airport shuttle may suit different arrival plans. Tell the provider if a late flight could affect collection.

Read the rental conditions

Check the driver requirements, deposit, accepted payment method, mileage, insurance excess and fuel policy. Compare equivalent cover and fees when looking at two prices.

Compare a car within a package

If you also need a flight or hotel, check whether the provider offers a suitable combined booking. Confirm what is included and compare the final price for the same dates and vehicle needs.
