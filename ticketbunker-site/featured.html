How the promotion works

Who receives the email?

Only verified subscribers who explicitly opt in to TicketBunker weekly picks. Your event must match both their selected location and at least one music style or interest. Unsubscribed contacts are excluded, and every promotional email includes an unsubscribe link. We do not promise an audience size or ticket-sales result.

When does the promotion start?

Choose a saved event in your organiser portal. Your event must be published, have tickets available and have its promotion payment confirmed before featured placement starts. Weekly emails include upcoming matches; cancelled, ended, sold-out and test events are excluded. Saving a request does not take payment.

What should I prepare?

A clear event poster, your promoter logo, event description, date, venue and ticket types. These are taken from your event setup so the promotion matches your branding.

What if the event changes?

Contact bookings@theticketbunker.com before the campaign is sent if the date, venue or event status changes. A sent email cannot be recalled; any follow-up must reflect the corrected event details.
