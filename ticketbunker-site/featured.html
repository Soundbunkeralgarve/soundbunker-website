How the promotion works

Who receives the email?

Only subscribers who opted in to TicketBunker and SoundBunker event news. Unsubscribed contacts are excluded, and every promotional email includes an unsubscribe link. We do not promise an audience size or ticket-sales result.

When does the promotion start?

Choose a saved event in your organiser portal. Your event must be published, have a working ticket link and have its promotion payment confirmed before placement or an email can be activated.

What should I prepare?

A clear event poster, your promoter logo, event description, date, venue and ticket types. These are taken from your event setup so the promotion matches your branding.

What if the event changes?

Contact bookings@soundbunker.pt before the campaign is sent if the date, venue or event status changes. A sent email cannot be recalled; any follow-up must reflect the corrected event details.
