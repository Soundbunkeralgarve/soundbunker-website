Events & activities in London

Start with people, budget and dates

Agree a realistic per-person budget and who is definitely coming. Include travel, accommodation, activities, meals and local transport. Check payment deadlines and what happens if someone drops out before the group commits.

Mix the headline plan with breathing space

Choose a main daytime activity and an evening plan, then leave time for arrivals, check-in and food. Cocktail classes, boat trips, live shows and club nights depend on destination, season and provider availability.

Use specialists for group weekends

StagWeb and GoHen offer their own destination and group booking services. Follow their links to discuss the trip and get current quotes. Each organiser confirms its own inclusions, payment arrangements, terms and accommodation rules.

Your questions.

Who books and runs a specialist weekend?

The named specialist handles its own enquiry, quote, booking and delivery. TicketBunker does not sell the linked accommodation and travel as a single combined holiday.

Can we add an event ticket and arrange the other parts separately?

Yes. Check the event’s age and group-entry rules, then use the hotel, dining and travel directories to arrange the other components with their providers. Each booking has separate terms.
