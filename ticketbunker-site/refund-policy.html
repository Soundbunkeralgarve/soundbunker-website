REFUNDS & CANCELLATIONS

Clear answers.
Fair refunds.

Plans change. Events sometimes do too. Here is how we handle requests, what depends on your booking, and where to get help.

Updated 21 November 2026. Read this guide alongside the refund terms and the conditions supplied with your booking. Earlier bookings retain their agreed terms. Your mandatory consumer rights always apply.

Start with your booking

Dated event tickets

Tickets for a specific event date or period are normally non-refundable if you change your mind, book by mistake, cannot attend, arrive late or miss the event. An organiser or supplier may offer a more flexible cancellation or amendment option; check the terms shown before you pay.

The usual 14-day change-of-mind withdrawal right generally does not apply to dated leisure activities under applicable EU and UK rules. Cancellation, material change and service-failure rights are separate.

Cinema vouchers & open-date products

An open-date voucher is not automatically subject to the dated-event rule. Check its cancellation, withdrawal, expiry, cinema and country restrictions, and whether you must redeem it or reserve a seat separately.

A voucher code is not necessarily a confirmed film screening or seat reservation. The product's rules must be explained before payment.

Who sold the booking?

The seller, activity provider, full price, currency and cancellation terms must be clear before payment. If you follow a link and pay on another seller's website, that seller handles the booking and its refund process. Keep their confirmation. TicketBunker's automatic event-cancellation refunds do not apply to another seller's checkout.

For supplier bookings sold through TicketBunker, contact us about the sale. We will check the terms agreed at purchase and coordinate with the responsible supplier where needed. Supplier conditions cannot remove your mandatory rights or TicketBunker's own legal obligations.

When the event changes

Cancelled events booked directly with TicketBunker

When TicketBunker confirms a whole-event cancellation for an event booked and ticketed directly through us, affected paid bookings are queued for a full automatic refund to the original payment method. You do not need to open a separate refund request for that cancellation. The purchaser and recorded ticket holders receive cancellation notices; payment-provider confirmation is separate.

Postponements, venue changes & other problems

For a change of date, time or venue, replacement of the programme or principal artists, or an interrupted event, the remedy depends on the booking, applicable law and circumstances. We will explain the options. You do not have to accept credit, a voucher or a replacement where the law entitles you to money back.

For artistic events in Portugal, statutory ticket-refund rules apply, including rules for changes and interruptions and their legal exceptions. An organiser's decision or a “no refunds” notice cannot override those rights.

If a ticket is missing, appears invalid, you believe you were wrongly refused entry, or the event was not supplied as described, tell the venue or organiser as soon as reasonably possible and raise a support case. Attending or having a ticket scanned does not automatically defeat a valid complaint. Equally, an unscanned ticket is not automatically refundable.

A problem at the door? Speak to venue staff straightaway. In your support message, include the event, venue, start time and what happened. Keep your QR code private and never send a password or full card number.

Request help or a refund review

  1. Use the booking account. Sign in to My tickets with the email used to book. The original purchaser can use the available refund tools. If you hold a transferred ticket or cannot access the account, contact support so we can check the records.
  2. Open one case for the issue. Use Customer support or email bookings@theticketbunker.com. Give the event name, order reference if available and a clear description. If a voluntary cancellation deadline applies, submit your request before it; mandatory rights remain protected.
  3. Keep the conversation together. Reply to the same case with any further information. We may need to verify the purchaser, payment, ticket status or relevant evidence. A request does not itself cancel a ticket or approve a refund; we will confirm the decision and its effect on the booking.

We review booking mistakes, payment errors and event complaints on their facts. No refund decision should be assumed from a support acknowledgement. You do not need to send unnecessary identity or medical documents to explain a general enquiry.

Understand your refund progress

  1. Requested / under review: the claim is being checked. No refund has been approved or paid yet.
  2. Approved: a decision has been made. This does not mean the money has reached your account.
  3. Submitted to the payment provider: processing has been requested. It may still be pending or fail.
  4. Processing result confirmed: check the confirmation and allow for your bank's processing. Contact us on the same case if the refund fails or remains outstanding.

Approved refunds normally use the original payment method. A full ticket refund is the amount actually paid for the affected ticket, including its applicable tax and after any discount. Any partial refund will be identified as such. Free tickets have no cash purchase amount to refund.

TicketBunker does not add a buyer booking fee. Organiser cancellation charges and unrecovered processing costs are not deducted from the full buyer refunds described above. Refunded or cancelled tickets cannot be used, and the same payment cannot be reimbursed twice. We check existing refunds and payment disputes to prevent duplicate repayment.

Processing and bank-arrival times vary. Mandatory refund deadlines still apply. A closed support case is not proof that a refund was paid; the payment result must be confirmed separately.

If you need a complaint review

Reply to your case or email bookings@theticketbunker.com with the reference, the issue and the outcome you are asking for. Ask for a complaint review if you disagree with a decision or the matter remains unresolved. We will explain the decision and next steps.

Our internal process does not restrict your statutory remedies, legitimate payment disputes or access to a competent consumer body or court. Official information is available through Portugal's Livro de Reclamações and the European Commission's consumer dispute resolution directory. The appropriate body depends on the dispute.
