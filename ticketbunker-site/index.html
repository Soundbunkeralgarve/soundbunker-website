About the TicketBunker

For the fans.
For the show.

Ticketing should help make events happen, not make them more expensive for everyone.

We believe ticket buyers shouldn't be punished with hidden booking charges and event organisers shouldn't have to give up a percentage of their hard-earned ticket sales.

That's why we've built TicketBunker around one transparent upfront organiser fee and zero TicketBunker booking fees at checkout. Your event. Your crowd. Your rules.

01 /

No buyer fee

Customers see the advertised ticket price without an extra TicketBunker booking charge.

02 /

No sales commission

Organisers retain ticket revenue after processing charges and relevant payment adjustments.

03 /

Fair access

Named tickets, verified account access and secure ownership transfers. A transferred ticket receives a new QR code.

Do buyers pay a TicketBunker booking fee?

No. TicketBunker does not add a booking fee to the advertised ticket price.

Can I send an event to friends?

Yes. Open the event page and choose Share event or WhatsApp. Each event has its own link, so friends can go directly to its details.

Where are age limits and access details?

These are set by the organiser and belong in the event description. Check before booking and contact the organiser if you need more information.

How our fees work

There are no TicketBunker booking fees added for buyers and no percentage sales commission charged by TicketBunker to organisers. Organisers pay an agreed upfront flat fee. Stripe's applicable payment-processing fees and payout adjustments, refunds or chargebacks are separate.
