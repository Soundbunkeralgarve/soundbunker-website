A few things worth knowing.

What do I pay as an organiser?

Choose the fixed band for your capacity: €99 up to 100 tickets, €199 up to 500, €299 up to 1,000, or €399 up to 2,000. Events over 2,000 pay €99 setup upfront plus €0.15 per paid ticket sold from the first sale, settled after the event. All prices include applicable IVA. Payment processing is separate. See full pricing and event bundles.

When can I start selling?

Create your account and prepare your event first. Sales require completed organiser verification, payment setup and the applicable listing fee. Your portal shows any outstanding steps.

Can I promote my event beyond its own page?

Yes. Use event sharing and street-team links, or request the optional featured package with homepage placement and weekly emails matched to subscribers’ locations and music tastes. Availability, payment and campaign approval apply; ticket sales are not guaranteed.

Does a transfer create a duplicate ticket?

No. An eligible ownership transfer replaces the previous ticket QR. The old code is invalidated; it does not become a second admission. Tickets should only be passed on at the original price or less. Private resale payments are not handled or verified by TicketBunker.

For the fans.
For the show.

Ticketing should help make events happen, not make them more expensive for everyone.

We believe ticket buyers shouldn't be punished with hidden booking charges and event organisers shouldn't have to give up a percentage of their hard-earned ticket sales.

That's why we've built TicketBunker around transparent organiser pricing and zero TicketBunker booking fees at checkout. Your event. Your crowd. Your rules.

Do buyers pay a TicketBunker booking fee?

No. TicketBunker does not add a booking fee to the advertised ticket price.

Are tickets refundable?

Normally not for changed plans or a missed event. Cancellations, event changes and genuine problems are covered by our refund policy and your legal rights. A refund request is reviewed, not automatically paid. Paid face-value resale is coming later and is not available yet.

Can I send an event to friends?

Yes. Open the event page and choose Share event or WhatsApp. Each event has its own link, so friends can go directly to its details.

Where are age limits and access details?

These are set by the organiser and belong in the event description. Check before booking and contact the organiser if you need more information.

How our fees work

There are no TicketBunker booking fees added for buyers and no percentage sales commission charged by TicketBunker to organisers. Organisers pay a capacity-band fee; events over 2,000 capacity pay a setup fee plus a fee for each paid ticket sold. Stripe's applicable payment-processing fees and payout adjustments, refunds or chargebacks are separate.
