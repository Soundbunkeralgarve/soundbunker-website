Good to know

Straight answers.

Still stuck? Open a support case ↗

Are there booking fees?

Not on TicketBunker tickets: the price you see is the price you pay, with applicable VAT / IVA included. Tickets sold by a partner follow that partner’s prices and fees, shown on their site before you pay.

Where are my tickets?

In your confirmation email and in My tickets. You can print them or, where eligible, add them to Apple or Google Wallet.

Can I give my ticket to a friend?

Where the organiser allows it, use a name transfer in My tickets. A new QR is issued and the old one stops working, so only one ticket is valid at the door.

What if the event is cancelled or I can’t go?

See the refund guide for what applies to your ticket, and open a support case from your booking if you need help.

Who am I buying from?

Cards marked “No booking fee” are sold through TicketBunker for the named organiser. Cards marked “via” a partner, such as “via Skiddle”, take you to that partner’s own site to buy: they sell and deliver the ticket, their terms apply, their booking or service fees may be added, and we may earn commission. Our no-booking-fee promise covers TicketBunker tickets only.
