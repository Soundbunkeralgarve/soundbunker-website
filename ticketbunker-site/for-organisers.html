KEEP THE QUEUE MOVING

Internet drops.
Your doors stay connected.

Prepare your event while online. The laptop Hub then checks downloaded tickets with compatible scanner apps over the same private Wi-Fi — without relying on venue internet.

  • One shared entry record across your paired door phones.
  • Local ticket checks when the internet drops.
  • Saved check-ins upload when you reconnect.

Wi-Fi stays on. Keep the laptop powered and every phone connected to the Hub. A phone that loses its Hub connection pauses approvals until it reconnects.

  1. 01 / BEFORE THE EVENT

    Download.

    Install the Hub and download your event to the laptop while online.

  2. 02 / AT THE VENUE

    Connect.

    Put the laptop and scanner phones on one private Wi-Fi. Pair each phone with its QR.

  3. 03 / AT THE DOOR

    Scan. Sync later.

    Check downloaded tickets locally. Reconnect to the internet to upload saved entries.

A FEW QUICK ANSWERS

Good to know.

What does it cost?

Choose an event plan for your capacity. Larger events use a deposit plus additional paid-ticket fees. Payment processing and optional extras are separate. See full pricing and the calculator →

When do I get paid?

Your event report runs automatically after the show. Standard bank arrival is normally 2–3 working days from release. Eligible Fast Payout is optional, with the fee shown before you choose. Follow the confirmed payout status in your portal.

What about scanning and poor signal?

Use the web scanner with internet. The early access Hub is available for offline testing: download your event beforehand and connect compatible scanner apps to the same private Wi-Fi. Test the complete setup before using it at an event. App downloads and setup guidance are in your Organiser Portal.

Can I change or postpone an event?

Eligible postponements can be requested up to 48 hours before the event, with the charge shown in your portal. Buyers can keep their booking or request a full refund. Add the new date and repost the linked event when ready.
