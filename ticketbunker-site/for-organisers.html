FROM YOUR FIRST IDEA TO THE FINAL SCAN

How your event comes together.

Prepare everything in the portal, open sales once your account is ready, then use the same workspace to manage your crew and review the results.

01

Set up your organisation.

Create your organiser profile, add your logo and billing information, and complete secure business and bank verification. Save your progress and come back with the same login.

02

Build your event page.

Add the poster, event description, headliner, venue, local start and finish times, and any age or access requirements. Set up your ticket tiers, prices and allocations before publishing.

03

Choose your plan. Open sales.

Review your event capacity, listing fee and any optional services. Sales open after organiser verification, payment setup and the required activation steps are complete.

04

Get the word out.

Share your event link across your channels. Use promo codes and street-team tools where enabled, and choose featured placement to reach more people browsing TicketBunker.

05

Get your door crew ready.

Assign authorised staff, prepare guest lists and make sure tickets can be scanned before doors open. For poor-signal venues, download the event to the Check-in Hub and pair compatible scanner phones.

06

Review the show. Track your payout.

After the recorded finish time, event reporting runs automatically. Review sales, check-ins, unscanned paid tickets and payout status, with confirmed deductions and bank arrival shown as they are reconciled.

BEFORE YOUR FIRST EVENT

Your questions, answered.

Where do I create and manage an event?

The Organiser Portal is your separate workspace for event setup, ticket tiers, crew, guest lists, branding and reports. Your buyers use the Tickets page and My tickets.

What does the event fee cover?

The standard event fee covers the capacity band you choose. Prices include applicable VAT / IVA. Payment processing, optional promotions, Fast Payout, refunds and disputes are separate. Over 2,000 tickets uses the deposit plus additional sales plan shown above.

Can I book several events?

Pre-book four events for 5% off base listing fees, or eight for 10% off. See the full pricing and reservation details for eligible plans and terms.

When will I receive my event proceeds?

Funds are held until after the event. The portal shows your post-event report, reconciliation and payout status. Standard bank arrival is normally 2–3 working days from release. Eligible Fast Payout is optional, carries a disclosed fee and depends on the bank destination. The confirmed status and expected arrival in your account are the figures to follow.

Do I need the app to scan tickets?

Authorised staff can use the web scanner with internet. Native scanner downloads are enabled when publicly available. The local Check-in Hub requires compatible scanner apps, one powered laptop and the same private Wi-Fi across the crew. Download the event before doors open.

What do buyers receive after booking?

Confirmed buyers receive secure ticket links by email and can find their bookings in My tickets. Printable tickets and eligible Apple Wallet or Google Wallet options are available with their booking. They should bring the current valid QR code and check the event’s entry requirements.

Is promotion included automatically?

Your published event can appear in the standard ticket listings. Featured placement is an optional service, separate from the event fee. It starts only after the required activation and payment confirmation. Weekly emails go only to opted-in subscribers with relevant matches; audience size and ticket sales are not guaranteed.

Do buyers pay a booking fee?

No added TicketBunker buyer booking fee. Your audience pays the advertised ticket price.
