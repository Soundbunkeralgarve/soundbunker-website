FAIR TICKETING. FINALLY.

Built for events.

At The Ticket Bunker, we believe ticketing should be simple, transparent and fair.

We believe fans shouldn’t pay booking fees just for buying a ticket, and event organisers shouldn’t have to give away a chunk of their revenue to sell one.

So we’ve changed the rules.

Your customers pay the advertised ticket price. No surprise booking charges at checkout. Organisers get clear, upfront pricing, with a fixed event fee up to 2,000 tickets and a published deposit plus additional sales plan for larger events.

Payment processing and optional services are separate, with the full details on our organiser pricing page.

Built for Events. Not for Taking Your Profits.

Whether you’re running a club night, live music show, comedy event, community gathering or independent festival, The Ticket Bunker gives you the professional tools to sell tickets, manage your event, track sales, reward your promoters and control entry — all from one easy-to-use platform.

We want organisers to spend less on ticketing and more on what actually matters: putting on incredible events.
