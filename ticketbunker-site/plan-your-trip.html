Events & activities in London

Start with the date that matters

A gig, theatre show or festival can set the timing for your whole trip. Check the venue, finish time and age restrictions before booking travel. Our city pages bring together the event listings and experiences currently available.

Keep the practical bits together

Compare the final hotel price, arrival time and journey home. A room near your venue may save a late taxi, while an airport transfer can help a group arriving together. Book each part with the named provider and keep its confirmation.

Build your own pace

Add one or two activities around the main event, leaving room for food, check-in and getting across the city. For stag and hen groups, confirm guest numbers and venue group policies before paying.

Your questions.

Where do I pay?

TicketBunker event tickets use the event’s own booking flow. Travel, hotel, dining and specialist group links take you to the named provider, which handles its booking, payment and terms.

Does every service operate in every city?

No. Search your exact route, pickup address, dates and party size with the provider. A directory link is a way to check options, not a confirmed quote or availability.
