How reporting and payouts work

The detailed report is generated automatically after the recorded finish time, normally within a minute. You do not need to submit or complete a report. Paid revenue, refunds and attendance remain separate. Unscanned paid tickets still count as sales.

Standard: no TicketBunker speed surcharge. Payout starts automatically after the show, the report and payment reconciliation. Standard bank arrival is normally 2–3 working days from release. The actual bank-arrival date appears when Stripe confirms it.

Optional fast payout: 1% of the instant payout, deducted from your proceeds with your consent, with no TicketBunker markup. Choose it before your event ends; an eligible bank destination is required. Eligible instant payouts typically arrive within 30 minutes of Stripe accepting them, after the same post-show checks.

Available payout options and any outstanding checks appear in your report. The 12-hour report time is separate from bank arrival.

Run a simple offline test

  1. Install the hub on one laptop and the scanner APK on two test phones. Sign in, choose a dedicated test event and download it in the hub while online.
  2. Connect the laptop and phones to the same private Wi-Fi. Pair each staff phone using its own QR in the hub, then confirm the event on both phones.
  3. Disconnect the router from the internet but keep Wi-Fi on. Scan the same test ticket on both phones: one should show APPROVED and the other ALREADY SCANNED. A phone that loses contact with the hub should pause approval.
  4. Restore internet, finish and sync the test event in the hub, then compare the organiser report with the hub totals. Keep the hub and scanner data until all entries have synced.

2. Open your event in the hub

Open installed hub app →

Sign in with this organiser account in the hub and download the selected event. Each laptop keeps its own admission record. Keep using the same hub until you finish and sync the event.

3. Connect the door team

  1. Connect the laptop and every scanner phone to the same private Wi-Fi router. The router can work without an internet connection.
  2. Invite your crew in Scanner Crew. In the hub, select each staff member and show their private pairing QR.
  3. On each updated scanner app, choose Connect to local hub and scan that pairing QR.
  4. Check that every phone shows the correct event and hub connection. Test the setup at the venue before opening doors.

Keep the hub laptop open, plugged in and connected to Wi-Fi. Allow private-network access if the computer asks. Guest Wi-Fi with device isolation blocks scanner connections.

During and after the event

With internet: entries are approved locally, then uploaded automatically. Newly issued tickets appear after the hub refreshes its download.

Without internet: downloaded tickets still work across all paired phones. New sales, refunds and staff changes need internet to reach the hub. Keep that in mind if you continue selling tickets elsewhere.

If the local Wi-Fi connection fails: the affected scanner pauses approvals until it reconnects. This protects against two doors admitting the same ticket.

When doors close: reconnect internet, choose Finish event and release tickets in the hub, and wait for all saved entries to upload. Reports show pending sync until the hub is finished.
