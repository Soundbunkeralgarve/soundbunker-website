AFTER THE APPLAUSE

Your report. Your payout.

Your automatic report becomes available 12 hours after the event's recorded finish time. All times are shown in the event's local timezone.

01 / SHOW FINISHES

Sales and attendance recorded

Paid tickets, gross revenue, refunds and staff scans are kept separately. Uns­canned paid tickets still count as sales.

02 / FINISH + 12 HOURS

Your post-show report

The report is generated automatically. The payout section shows the reconciled amount, deductions, chosen payout method and Stripe's expected arrival date when available.

03 / STANDARD

No TicketBunker speed surcharge

Funds are released only after the show, the report and the required checks. Bank arrival depends on available funds, Stripe and your bank; the confirmed date appears here.

OPTIONAL / FAST PAYOUT

Stripe's cost. No added markup.

Indicative fee: 1% of the payout (€10 per €1,000). The exact fee must be confirmed before payment. Eligible instant payouts typically arrive within 30 minutes of Stripe accepting the payout, after the post-show checks.

Awaiting live Stripe activation

A fast payout is shown as paid only after verified payment. It cannot release money before the event or bypass refunds and account checks.

The 12-hour report is not a promise that money has reached your bank. No payout amount or arrival date is invented while reconciliation or Stripe confirmation is pending.
