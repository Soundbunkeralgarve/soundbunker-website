How reporting and payouts work

The report is generated automatically 12 hours after the recorded finish time. Paid revenue, refunds and attendance remain separate. Unscanned paid tickets still count as sales.

Standard: no TicketBunker speed surcharge. Funds are released after the show, the report and reconciliation. The actual bank-arrival date appears when Stripe confirms it.

Optional fast payout: indicative 1% Stripe fee at cost, with no TicketBunker markup (€10 per €1,000). The exact fee and eligibility must be confirmed before payment. Eligible instant payouts typically arrive within 30 minutes of Stripe accepting them, after the same post-show checks.

Live payment release and fast payout are awaiting Stripe activation. The 12-hour report is not a promise of bank receipt at that time.
