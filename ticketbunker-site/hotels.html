Events & activities in London

Location beats a vague “nearby” label

Check the hotel’s map against the actual venue and your arrival station or airport. Look at the journey after the event finishes as well as the daytime route. Our hotel search starts with the city; the provider’s map lets you narrow down the location.

Compare the whole stay

Set the right number of guests and rooms, then check taxes, breakfast, deposits, check-in times and cancellation conditions. The cheapest headline room may have different terms from a flexible booking.

Book a group with the right conditions

For stag, hen and birthday weekends, confirm that the accommodation accepts your group type and room allocation. Ask about deposits, noise policies and the latest check-in before committing to the stay.
