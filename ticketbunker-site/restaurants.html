Leave enough time before the show

Compare the restaurant address with the venue, then allow time to order, eat and get through the doors. Tell the restaurant if you have a fixed finish time and check kitchen opening hours for a meal after the event.

Read the offer details

Offers depend on the restaurant, date, time and menu. Drinks, set menus or particular items may be excluded. Confirm any offer through the restaurant or booking provider; TicketBunker does not promise a discount on every table.

Make group requirements clear

Ask about maximum party size, deposits, set menus and dietary requirements. Stag and hen groups should check the restaurant’s group policy before booking. A restaurant may need to confirm a large table separately.
