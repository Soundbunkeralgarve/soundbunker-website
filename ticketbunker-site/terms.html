1. Your event and your ticket

The event organiser identified on the event listing is responsible for delivering the event and its venue, schedule, admission rules and event-specific information. Read those details before booking. TicketBunker provides the ticketing platform and account tools.

Your ticket is valid for its named event, date and ticket type. One valid QR admits one person once. Keep your ticket link and QR private. The venue may check age, identification and other clearly stated admission requirements. Copying a ticket does not create an extra admission.

2. Accounts and ticket transfers

Use a genuine email address and accurate attendee information. Access to account tools requires a verified email. Keep your password secure and contact support if your account is compromised.

Eligible unused tickets can be renamed or transferred before the event using My tickets. Transfers move ownership to the recipient's verified email account and replace the previous QR. The platform currently limits each ticket to five ownership/name updates. A transfer does not collect a resale payment or transfer the original purchaser's payment record. Do not rely on a forwarded screenshot as proof of ownership.

3. Refunds, cancellations and changes

The original purchaser can submit a refund request from My tickets or contact support with the ticket reference and reason. Requests are reviewed against the event circumstances, the terms shown at purchase and applicable consumer law. Submitting a request is not confirmation that a refund has been approved or paid.

For cancelled or materially changed events, contact support for the available remedy. Approved payment refunds are returned through the payment provider to the original payment method where supported; bank processing times vary. Refunded or cancelled tickets cannot be used for entry. Nothing in these terms removes mandatory consumer rights.

4. Ticket prices and fees

TicketBunker does not add a buyer booking fee. The final price, currency and any applicable tax must be shown before payment. A booking is confirmed only when payment is confirmed and the ticket is issued. Live ticket checkout is currently awaiting payment activation.

5. Organiser services and reporting

Organisers must provide accurate event details, a finish time, the necessary permissions for artwork, and complete account and tax verification before selling. Listing prices are based on total ticket capacity: €59 / £49 up to 100 tickets, €119 / £99 up to 500, and €239 / £199 up to 2,000, plus applicable tax. Larger events require a quote. Charity listing exemptions require verification. TicketBunker charges 0% ticket commission; payment-provider fees are separate.

The automatic post-show report is scheduled 12 hours after the recorded event finish time and normally generated within the following minute. It records paid ticket revenue and attendance separately. Paid tickets that were not scanned still count as sales. The report is available in the organiser workspace; it is not a bank-payment confirmation.

The planned payout service releases eligible funds after the show, the report and reconciliation. The workspace displays a net amount only after reconciliation, and a bank-arrival date only when Stripe provides one. Refunds, disputes, available funds, account checks and banking delays may affect release. This service is awaiting live Stripe activation and is not an escrow service.

Optional fast payout is intended for eligible accounts after the same post-show checks. The indicative fee is Stripe's 1% instant payout cost, with no additional TicketBunker markup; the exact amount must be confirmed before payment. Typically, an eligible instant payout arrives within 30 minutes after Stripe accepts it, but this is not guaranteed. Paying for speed does not bypass verification, refunds or the post-show hold.

6. Featured events and promotion

The featured package costs €59 / £49 per event, plus applicable tax, subject to availability and approval. It includes one featured placement and one promotional email to the opted-in SoundBunker events audience. A request does not take payment or send an email. Delivery requires a published event, verified payment and activated campaign sending. No audience size, ticket sales or campaign result is guaranteed.

7. Privacy and your choices

SoundBunker Algarve processes account contact details, event and ticket records, payment references, attendee names, transfers, refund requests and entry scans to provide the service, protect accounts, support customers and meet applicable obligations. Payment details are handled by Stripe; TicketBunker does not ask you to send card details by email.

Service providers include Supabase for account and database services, Vercel for hosting, Stripe for payments and Resend for email. Necessary event and attendee information is available to the responsible organiser and authorised door staff. We use service records to fulfil bookings, maintain security and meet legal obligations. Records are kept for as long as needed for these purposes and applicable retention requirements.

Marketing is optional and requires a separate choice. You can withdraw consent in My tickets or by using the unsubscribe link in a promotional email. Buying a ticket does not automatically subscribe you. Essential account and ticket messages are separate from marketing.

The account uses browser storage to maintain your sign-in session. Third-party fonts and event artwork may be loaded when a page is opened. Contact bookings@soundbunker.pt to request access, correction, deletion or portability of your personal data, or to raise a privacy concern. Identity verification may be needed. You may also complain to your competent data-protection authority, including Portugal's CNPD.

8. Help

For booking, ticket, payment or account help, contact bookings@soundbunker.pt. Include your ticket or event reference and never send a password or full card number.
