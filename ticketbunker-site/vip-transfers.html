Stretched limos, chauffeur cars or airport transfers?

Choose the type of journey you want. A stretched limousine must be explicitly listed by the supplier; a luxury sedan or van is a different vehicle. Check the actual model or class, passenger and luggage capacity, hire duration, waiting time and child-seat options before booking.

Give the driver the right details

Use the flight number for an airport collection and the full venue or hotel address for a drop-off. Allow time after an event to reach the agreed pickup point, especially when roads around a venue are closed.

Confirm the quote before the journey

Coverage and vehicle availability vary by city. The provider confirms the final fare, inclusions, cancellation policy and passenger instructions. A search link does not reserve a car or guarantee a particular vehicle.

Your questions.

Where do I book and pay?

Viator links open a tracked search. You choose the vehicle or service, pay and receive confirmation through Viator under the listing’s terms. Blacklane and Welcome Pickups links open those providers’ public sites. The named supplier operates the journey.

Can I book a stretched limo in every city?

Search is available for every city in our directory, but a search link does not confirm stock. Check that the actual listing specifies a stretched limousine, your dates and the full party size. Some destinations may offer chauffeur cars or airport transfers instead.

Can a large stag or hen group use a chauffeur service?

Check the provider’s passenger and luggage limits and its group policy. A larger group may need more than one vehicle or a different transfer service.
