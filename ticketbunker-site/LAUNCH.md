# TicketBunker update — 19 October 2026

## Current changes
- Buyer and organiser routes have consistent Buy tickets / Sell tickets navigation, purpose labels and distinct account wording. Public shop, account, event, checkout, pricing and promotion pages share the route switch. The organiser workspace remains separate.
- Correct TicketBunker support address across all standalone pages; repaired promotion and organiser-benefit links; mobile and print rules retained.
- TicketBunker transactional email body uses explicit white backgrounds and black text with light-mode metadata. Individual email apps may still apply their own dark-mode rendering.
- Automatic event report is due at the recorded finish time and generated by the existing minute scheduler. It includes sales, gross revenue, refunded orders, paid check-ins, unscanned paid tickets, ticket tiers and scanner activity. Downloads include financial deductions and provider-confirmed payout details.
- Reconciled standard payouts start automatically; normally allow 2–3 working days from release, with the provider date shown once confirmed.
- Organisers can select eligible Fast Payout before the event ends and consent to a 1% fee on the instant payout, deducted from proceeds without markup. Only eligible destinations and instant balances can be used. Unavailable instant funds queue a retry without silently substituting standard payout. Legacy prepaid fast fees are not deducted again.
- Existing verification, refund/dispute holds, source-charge reconciliation, release lock, provider-history recovery and webhook confirmation remain in place. No manual report submission is required.
