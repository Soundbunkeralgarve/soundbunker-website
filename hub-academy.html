A working studio that teaches

Creativity becomes confidence.

The Hub Academy brings music, art, media and urban sport together in the rooms where creative work happens. Young people learn from working practitioners, using real equipment and making things they can show, play or perform.

No experience needed. Every session begins with curiosity and gives each person a way to take part.

From a first session to a finished track, a portfolio or a new skill — the work belongs to them.

After school · Creative Clubs

Find your thing.

Three ways in, one creative home. Weekly, practical clubs for all abilities, with no special equipment or experience needed.

CLUB 01 · MUSIC & AUDIO

Find your sound.

Write, perform, record and produce in a working studio. Make a track that is really yours.

  • Singing, rap and vocal performance
  • Songwriting and beat-making
  • Recording, production and DJ skills
  • Podcasting and voice-over
Take it home: music you helped create.
CLUB 02 · ART & MEDIA

Make something to be proud of.

Design, shoot, paint and publish. Build a real portfolio beyond a folder of exercises.

  • Street art and responsible graffiti
  • Drawing, lettering and mural design
  • Photography, video and editing
  • Graphic design and exhibitions
Take it home: creative work worth showing.
CLUB 03 · MOVEMENT & URBAN SPORT

Move, land it, own it.

Find your balance on a board and your rhythm on the floor — from first try to new confidence.

  • Skate fundamentals and safety
  • Balance, tricks and progression
  • Breakdance and street dance
  • Routines, freestyle and cyphers
Take it home: the courage to try again.
