Explore · Observe · Guide · Develop

A place to find their thing.

Children do not have to arrive already knowing what they are good at. The Academy gives them space to try different creative disciplines, then helps them build confidence and go deeper where the strongest connection appears.

Activities rotate so every child gets exposure to different skills. The aim is not another classroom exercise: it is to make, record, film, paint, perform, move and finish something real.

No experience or equipment needed. All abilities are welcome.

Real creative spacesSessions take place at The Hub Culture, SoundBunker’s professional studio and, when appropriate, Loulé Skate Park.
Working practitionersYoung people learn with people who actively work in music, media, art and urban sport.
Visible progressCollectable patches and progression stripes reward effort, skills, teamwork and personal development.

Three doors into one Academy

What they can explore.

Every child gets the chance to try music and audio, art and digital media, and movement and urban sport.

01 · MUSIC & AUDIO

Find your sound.

Write, perform, record and produce in a working studio.

  • Singing, rap and vocal performance
  • Songwriting and creative expression
  • Beat-making and music production
  • Professional studio recording
  • DJ skills and track selection
  • Podcasting, voice-over and sound tech
A finished track can become something they are genuinely proud to play back.
02 · ART & DIGITAL MEDIA

Make something to be proud of.

Design, shoot, paint and publish while building a real creative portfolio.

  • Responsible street art and lettering
  • Drawing, lettering and mural design
  • Photography and studio shoots
  • Video and content creation
  • Graphic design and visual storytelling
  • Exhibitions and group projects
Work goes on the wall, on screen or into a portfolio — not into a drawer.
03 · MOVEMENT & URBAN SPORT

Move, land it, own it.

Build balance, rhythm, coordination and confidence on the board and on the floor.

  • Skateboarding fundamentals
  • Balance, safety and progression
  • New tricks and first drop-in
  • Breakdance and street dance
  • Rhythm, coordination and freestyle
  • Group routines and cyphers
Falling over is part of it. So is getting back up.

The patch system

Earn it.
Wear it.

Just like scout badges, pupils collect patches as they build skills and complete challenges. Progress is recognised through effort and development, not simply natural talent.

Where it can lead

Keep growing when they find a spark.

Creative Clubs are the starting point. When a young person shows a strong interest, the Academy can help them develop it further.

01

Focused tuition

One-to-one or small-group development in production, recording, photography, video, skate and other specialist areas.

02

Workshops & masterclasses

Go deeper with experienced artists, producers, photographers, designers, skaters and dancers.

03

Real outcomes

A finished track, photo series, short film, exhibition, mural, routine or performance gives them something real to show for the work.

04

Family guidance

We can share where we see the strongest connection and suggest the next step without forcing a child into one path too early.
