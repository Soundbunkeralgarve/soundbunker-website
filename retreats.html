YOUR BASE: LOULÉ, PORTUGAL

A retreat with a town on its doorstep

Old-town character.
Fresh inspiration.

Based at SoundBunker and The Hub Culture in central Loulé, near the castle and the town’s landmark Municipal Market.

Step outside and the rhythm changes. Cobbled streets, small cafés, local restaurants and independent shops make the old town somewhere to explore between sessions. There is space to wander, get a coffee, fill a notebook or simply sit and let an idea settle.

The castle and historic centre give this part of the Algarve its own character. You are staying connected to everyday Portuguese life while making time for your own music — with the wider Algarve there to explore when your schedule allows.

Before you join us

A few useful details.

Who is the retreat for?

Artists, singers and songwriters who want dedicated time to develop their music with professional guidance and a supportive creative community. Tell the team where you are in your music journey so they can help you decide whether this edition is a good fit.

Where does it take place?

Our creative base is SoundBunker inside The Hub Culture, Rua Ataíde de Oliveira 27, 8100-269 Loulé, Algarve, Portugal. We are in the town centre, near the castle and Municipal Market. Accommodation and any additional performance venues are confirmed for each retreat.

What will I work on?

The partner programme combines songwriting, studio recording, workshops, content creation, rehearsal and a live showcase. The published retreat includes a fully written, mixed and mastered song and professional content. Confirm the detailed programme for your edition with Algarve Music Retreats.

How do accommodation and travel work?

Speak to Algarve Music Retreats about local accommodation, the room arrangements and what is included in your package. Their published retreat excludes flights, airport transfers and alcohol. Confirm your full booking details before arranging travel.

Can I pay in full or in instalments?

We plan to offer payment in full or automatic instalments through SoundBunker once the prices and payment schedule are finalised. These options are not open on this page yet. Contact the retreat team for current booking options.
