## Description (both stores)
Book it. Head out. theticketbunker brings gigs, club nights, comedy, theatre, festivals, family days out and tours across the UK, Ireland and Europe into one app with big, easy buttons.

FIND SOMETHING ON
• Search any town, city or region, or tap Near me
• Tonight, this weekend or the next 7 days
• 153 cities and islands with their own pages, plus a map
• Live music, club nights, comedy, festivals, theatre, family, cinema and days out

BOOK IT
• TicketBunker events: no booking fee, ever
• Partner events (such as Skiddle) open on the seller's own site. The seller is always named, and their fees may apply.

HEAD OUT
• Get to the venue: walk, bus/train/metro, Uber, Citymapper or the Tube map
• Rough walking and taxi times worked out on your phone
• My trip: plan tickets, travel, a bed, food and things to do for each night

YOUR TICKETS
• Sign in with your TicketBunker account to see your tickets and door QR codes
• Add tickets to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet
• TicketBunker VIP members see their perks and claim VIP guestlist spots

Works on iPhone, iPad and Android phones and tablets.

## App Privacy (Apple) / Data safety (Google)
- **Contact info, email:** linked to the user; used for app functionality and account management. Not used for tracking.
- **Identifiers, user ID:** linked; used for app functionality.
- **Purchases:** none in the app. Tickets are bought on the website or the partner's site.
- **Location:** NOT collected. Approximate location is used on the device only, when the user taps Near me or How far, and is never sent off the phone.
- **Tracking:** no. No advertising SDKs.
- **Encrypted in transit:** yes (HTTPS). **Account deletion:** yes, in the app (My tickets → Delete my account) and by email.

## App Review notes
This is the fan app for TicketBunker (theticketbunker.com), a ticketing platform for event organisers.
- **Demo account:** [buyer email] / [password]. It holds a valid ticket for [event], so you can see My tickets, the door QR code and the wallet buttons.
- **Partner listings:** events marked "Sold by Skiddle · fees may apply" open on the partner's website, which sells and supports those tickets. TicketBunker's own events open our web checkout for real-world event tickets (guideline 3.1.3(e)).
- **VIP:** TicketBunker VIP is not sold in the app. The app only shows perks to existing members, and the guestlist spots are for real-world events.
- **Location:** requested only when the user taps Near me or How far, and used on the device.
- **Account deletion:** My tickets → Delete my account.
