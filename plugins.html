SoundBunker Armoury

Plug-ins built in a working studio.

Synths, drums, saturation, mastering and a full mix toolkit, made and used at SoundBunker in Loulé. AU, VST3 and standalone for macOS, VST3 and standalone for Windows.

The 11-effect Armoury is free, and taking it unlocks Armoury prices: any other plug-in for €19, or every one of them for €49. Try any of them free for 14 days; one licence covers 2 computers, Mac and Windows mixed.

How it works

  1. Get the free Armoury, or download and try. Enter your e-mail for the free Armoury key. Install any other plug-in and press START 14-DAY TRIAL. After the trial it keeps working in demo mode, with a short silence every 45 seconds.
  2. Buy a licence. Pay securely by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. Your licence key arrives by e-mail straight away and is saved in your Client Area.
  3. Activate. Paste the key into the plug-in and press ACTIVATE. It needs the internet once; after that it works offline.
  4. Change computers any time. Press DEACTIVATE THIS COMPUTER in the plug-in, or remove an old computer in your Client Area, then activate on the new one.

Questions

What do I need?

macOS 12 or later (Apple silicon or Intel), or Windows 10/11 64-bit, and a DAW that loads AU or VST3 plug-ins (Logic, Cubase, Ableton Live, FL Studio, Studio One, Reaper and others). Every plug-in also runs on its own as a standalone app.

Does it need the internet?

Only to activate. The plug-in quietly re-checks its licence about once a month when it can; if your studio is offline it keeps working for 60 more days before asking you to connect.

Activation does not work inside GarageBand.

GarageBand keeps plug-ins away from the internet. Open the standalone app from Applications, activate there, and the plug-in in GarageBand is licensed too.

Can I get a refund?

Try the plug-in free for 14 days before buying. If something is wrong with a purchase, write to bookings@soundbunker.pt and we will sort it out.
