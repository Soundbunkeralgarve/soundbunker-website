THE MODEL

More event.
Less overhead.

Promoters manage their events and sell tickets using their own verified Stripe payment account. SoundBunker provides the platform, not a middleman holding ticket revenue.

01

Create your event

Set the venue, ticket types and capacity. Organiser verification and tax information will be required before paid tickets go live.

02

Pay once to publish

Choose a clear listing price for your event size. No percentage-based SoundBunker commission on every ticket sold.

03

Sell tickets directly

Ticket buyers pay the advertised ticket price. Card processing is charged by Stripe to the organiser.

04

Let the crowd in

Unique QR tickets, a phone-based entry scanner and attendance reporting are being built and tested.
