Who takes payment fees?

Stripe handles all card transactions and charges its payment-processing fees directly to the event organiser. These are similar to the processing charges a business pays when customers use a card machine. Stripe deducts its processing fees from the organiser's ticket receipts before bank payout.

ticketBunker does not hold or distribute ticket revenue. The organiser's own Stripe account receives the payments. We charge only the agreed flat event-listing fee — no percentage commission and no extra ticketBunker fee for the buyer.

Regular bank payouts follow the organiser's Stripe payout schedule. Optional faster payouts may involve additional Stripe fees and eligibility requirements; they are not guaranteed.

BETA: Prices shown are proposals. Checkout is currently disabled. GBP and EUR are separate plan prices; applicable taxes and Stripe payment-processing fees are additional. When ticket sales exceed a fixed-tier allowance, the organiser must approve and pay only the upgrade difference before further sales. Ticket counts are based on tickets sold, not merely the number created. Larger festival packages will be considered after the small-events launch.
