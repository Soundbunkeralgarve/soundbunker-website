S

Stripe processes the payments. ticketBunker doesn't handle ticket money.

Stripe handles all card transactions and charges card-processing fees directly to the organiser's own Stripe account. These are similar to a card-machine processing charge. Stripe deducts its processing fees from ticket receipts before bank payout. We charge a separate flat listing fee, with no extra ticketBunker buyer booking fee.

Standard payouts follow Stripe's schedule. Optional faster payouts may have their own Stripe fees and eligibility restrictions.

BETA pricing proposal — checkout is disabled. GBP and EUR prices are separate and applicable tax is extra. Ticket brackets refer to tickets sold, not ticket types created. Upgrading requires organiser approval, and only the difference between fixed-price brackets is due. Larger festivals will be considered after the initial small-event launch.
