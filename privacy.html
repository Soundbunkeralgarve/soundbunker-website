Privacy

SOUNDBUNKER ALGARVE, SOCIEDADE UNIPESSOAL LDA, NIF 517757478, uses customer information to manage bookings, payments, invoices, safety and service delivery.

Information we collect

Booking information may include your name, email address, telephone number, tax number, selected service, appointment details and notes you provide. Registration for activities involving children may also include guardian, emergency, collection, consent and relevant health information.

How information is used

Information is used only where needed to provide the requested service, communicate about the booking, process payment, issue accounting documents and meet legal obligations. Payment card details are processed by Stripe and are not stored by this website.

Services involved

Booking information may be processed through Vercel, Stripe, Google Calendar and InvoiceXpress for hosting, payment, scheduling and invoicing. Each service processes information under its own privacy and security terms.

Promotional emails

We add you to the promotions list only when you actively subscribe on the site. Resend processes the mailing list and provides an unsubscribe link in marketing messages. Booking confirmation emails are separate and are sent whether or not you subscribe.

Your choices

You may request access, correction or deletion of your information where applicable. Image consent for children is collected separately and may be withdrawn for future use.

Contact

Privacy requests can be sent to bookings@soundbunker.pt.

Shop orders

For merchandise, we collect your contact details, delivery address and order contents. Supabase stores order records, Stripe processes payment, Printful receives the information needed to produce and deliver your order, and Resend sends order confirmations. Your private order-status link provides access to your order summary and tracking; keep it private.
