# TicketBunker customer support and refund policy

Reviewed 21 November 2026. This document records the policy boundaries for the support workflow. It is not a new supplier agreement and does not activate supplier inventory.

## Customer pages

- `/support`: account-linked support cases and the published help route, implemented separately.
- `/refund-policy`: plain-language customer guide.
- `/terms#refunds`: contractual terms; earlier bookings keep the terms agreed at purchase.
- `/terms#privacy`: existing privacy notice, also reached through the `/privacy` redirect.

Retain the native whole-event cancellation flow and full affected buyer refunds. The organiser's £25 cancellation administration charge and actual unrecovered processing costs are separate organiser liabilities. Do not deduct them from the full buyer refunds promised in the current terms. This update does not change organiser pricing, payout or postponement rules.

## Decision rules

| Situation | Required handling |
| --- | --- |
| Specific-date leisure ticket; change of mind, mistaken booking, inability to attend or no-show | Usually no voluntary refund unless the disclosed booking conditions allow it. Do not treat this as excluding statutory cancellation or service-failure remedies. |
| Open-date cinema voucher, gift product or other undated product | Review its legal classification, withdrawal, expiry and redemption rules separately; do not automatically apply the dated-leisure exception. |
| Native whole-event cancellation | Preserve the existing automatic cancellation refund path, notices and payment-provider checks. |
| Postponement, venue/time change, principal artist or programme change, interruption | Check applicable law and the actual booking terms; offer the required remedy and do not force credit where a money refund is owed. |
| Missing/invalid ticket, incorrect refusal, wrong product, payment error | Human review of payment, booking, access and event evidence. A scan or lack of a scan is not an automatic decision. |
| External seller checkout | Identify that seller and its support/refund route clearly before the customer leaves to pay; do not imply native cancellation automation covers the external sale. |
| Supplier product sold through TicketBunker | TicketBunker owns its sales enquiry and coordinates with the supplier. Supplier approval/reimbursement cannot extinguish TicketBunker's own legal obligations. |
| Transferred ticket | Verify the holder and original purchaser/payment separately. Do not send a refund to an arbitrary contact or treat transfer as repayment. |
| Existing dispute/refund | Reconcile outstanding refundable amount and existing payment-provider state to prevent duplicate reimbursement; preserve legitimate consumer disputes. |

Before enabling a supplier checkout, store the seller/provider identity, product cancellation terms and policy version with the booking confirmation. Display total price/currency, date and destination, admission/redemption conditions, cancellation deadlines and the correct support route before payment. This document alone does not implement that booking snapshot or certify a supplier checkout.

## Case and payment states are different

The customer needs to understand the difference between a request, review/decision, payment-provider submission and the confirmed processing result. Closing a support case must not mark a payment as refunded. A failed or pending refund remains actionable until reconciled. Show only a provider-confirmed bank-arrival estimate, where one exists; never derive an arrival promise from approval time alone.

For manual financial actions, verify case/booking ownership, original payment, currency, already-refunded amount and open disputes. Record actor, reason, amount, decision and provider result. Use the existing authorised payment path; do not let a message, FAQ response or case status alone trigger a payment. Native reviewed event-wide automation remains separate.

## Operational service targets

These are internal starting targets, not public staffed-hour or 24/7 guarantees:

- First human response within one working day.
- Progress update at least every two working days while unresolved, including supplier waits.
- Put imminent admission and missing/invalid ticket issues at the front of the queue using the event's actual start time and timezone.
- Set an owner and next-action deadline for every unresolved case. Escalate overdue, failed-refund and complaint-review cases.

Publish narrower response promises only when staffing and escalation cover support them. Automated receipt acknowledgement confirms receipt only, not eligibility or resolution. A complaint review should explain the decision and next steps; internal review must not remove statutory remedies or impose an unlawful wait before external redress.

## Personal data

Collect only necessary messages, booking references and evidence. Keep staff notes and unrelated customers' records private. Do not request passwords, full payment-card details or unnecessary identity/health evidence. Apply authenticated ownership checks, role-based access, audit and retention/deletion rules. Use an appropriate service/legal/security basis, not marketing consent, for support processing. Marketing remains a separate optional choice.

## Primary sources and their limits

- **UK Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, regulation 28(1)(h)** — specific-date or specific-period leisure services are excluded from the ordinary distance-contract cancellation regime. This does not establish a universal exclusion for undated vouchers: <https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/3134/regulation/28/2020-12-31/data.html>.
- **EU Consumer Rights Directive 2011/83/EU, Article 16(l)** — equivalent dated-leisure withdrawal exception: <https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2011/83/oj/eng>. The Court of Justice's overview also addresses tickets sold through intermediaries: <https://curia.europa.eu/site/jcms/qua1_5955/en/consumer-rights>.
- **UK Consumer Rights Act 2015, section 56(4)–(6)** — refunds under that service-remedy section are due without undue delay, and no later than 14 days after the trader agrees entitlement; same means of payment unless the consumer expressly agrees otherwise; no refund fee. Do not describe this as a universal deadline for every EU/UK event claim: <https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/15/section/56/data.html>.
- **Portugal Decreto-Lei 23/2014, Article 9, republished in Decreto-Lei 90/2019** — event not held at the stated place/date/time, main programme/artist changes and interruptions can require restitution. The interruption provision has a limited post-start force-majeure exception. The 30-day rule in paragraph 5 follows notification of an IGAC decision; it is not a universal clock starting at cancellation: <https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/decreto-lei/90-2019-122960393>.
- **European Commission** — event-cancellation consequences depend on national contract law and the contract; supplier terms are not a substitute for that review. The linked explanation arose during COVID-19 and is not a current standalone cancellation code: <https://commission.europa.eu/topics/consumers/consumer-rights-and-complaints/resolve-your-consumer-complaint/european-consumer-centres-network-ecc-net/faq-cancellations-individually-booked-accommodations-car-rental-and-events-due-covid-19_en>.
- **EDPB small-business guide** — purpose limitation, data minimisation, security, lawful basis and retention apply to support records, including booking references: <https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/learn-the-basics/data-protection-basics_en>.
- **Current complaint information:** Portugal's <https://www.livroreclamacoes.pt/>; IGAC's artistic-event complaint information at <https://igac.gov.pt/espetaculos/reclamacoes>; the European Commission's <https://consumer-redress.ec.europa.eu/dispute-resolution-bodies>. Do not imply membership, registration or a specific body's jurisdiction without checking it.
- **EU ODR closure:** the old Online Dispute Resolution platform closed on 20 July 2025. Do not publish the old ODR complaint route: <https://consumer-redress.ec.europa.eu/site-relocation_en>.
