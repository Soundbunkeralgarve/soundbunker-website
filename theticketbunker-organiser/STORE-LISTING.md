## Description (both stores)
TicketBunker For Organisers is the free companion app for organisers on TicketBunker (theticketbunker.com). Clubs, bars, promoters, festivals, charities and private hosts use it to run their events from a phone.

CREATE AND PUBLISH
• Event details, start and end times in venue local time, and a poster from your photos or camera
• Ticket types with your own prices and quantities, up to 2,000 tickets self-service
• Optional VIP early access for TicketBunker VIP members
• See exactly what's left before you can publish, then publish

RUN YOUR EVENT
• Live numbers: paid tickets, revenue, door check-ins, no-shows, guest passes and door sales
• Share, copy or open your event page link
• Invite and revoke door staff, who scan with the free TicketBunker Scanner app
• Guest lists: give artists free passes, or add VIP guestlist spots

FAIR FOR YOUR CROWD
Your buyers pay the ticket price, with no TicketBunker booking fee added.

Account set-up, payouts and billing are completed on theticketbunker.com/organiser.
