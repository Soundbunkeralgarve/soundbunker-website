RECON app

RECON privacy policy

Last updated 1 November 2026.

What the app does with your recordings

RECON for iPhone, iPad and Android records audio only when you press record. Your takes are stored inside the app on your device. They leave the device only when you choose to: either by sending them to a computer you have paired with RECON on your own local network (encrypted, directly between your devices) or by sharing a .recon file yourself.

What SoundBunker collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not send your recordings or any other data to SoundBunker or to anyone else.

Permissions

Microphone: to record takes. Local network: to find and send takes to the computers you pair. You can withdraw either permission in your device settings at any time.

The RECON plug-in

The RECON sampler plug-in for macOS and Windows is covered by the SoundBunker privacy policy. When you activate it, it sends your licence key, a code identifying the computer, its name and operating system to soundbunker.pt to check the licence.
