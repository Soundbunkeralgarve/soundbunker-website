What should I do before going offline?

Sign into the scanner apps, download the event on this laptop, connect all devices to the same private Wi-Fi and pair each scanner. Run a test at the venue before doors open.

New sales, refunds and staff changes reach the Hub after an internet sync. The Hub reserves downloaded tickets so separate online scanners cannot admit them again.

Scan with the phone’s Camera and tap Join. Then open the Door Scanner app to pair. A “No internet” warning is normal on a local-only network: stay connected.

A phone won’t connect?

Check the Wi-Fi name and password, laptop firewall and the scanner app’s local-network permission. Avoid guest Wi-Fi that blocks devices from communicating. Keep the laptop open, powered and within Wi-Fi range.

What happens if the internet drops?

Downloaded tickets stay on this laptop. Paired phones continue checking them over local Wi-Fi, and check-ins stay saved until an internet sync succeeds. Keep this laptop and all scanners connected to the same local network.

If a phone loses its local connection to the Hub, that phone pauses approvals until it reconnects. The offline permit must remain valid. Finish the event once doors close and internet is available to upload remaining check-ins and release the tickets.
