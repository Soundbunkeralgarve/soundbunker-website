TicketBunker Check-in Hub — Mac testing build

Choose arm64 for Apple silicon (M1, M2, M3 or later), or x64 for Intel.
Open the disk image and drag TicketBunker Check-in Hub to Applications.
Eject the disk image, then open the app from Applications.

This test app has an ad-hoc integrity signature, not an Apple Developer ID
signature or Apple notarization. macOS may require an explicit one-time
approval in System Settings > Privacy & Security. Only approve it if you
trust this TicketBunker test release. Do not disable Gatekeeper, remove
quarantine attributes or turn off other Mac security protections.

If macOS says the app is damaged, will damage your computer or must be
deleted, stop and report the exact message. Do not override that warning.

Use a dedicated test event. Prepare it online before testing offline.
Connect the hub and scanner phones to the same private Wi-Fi.
Real venue and multi-phone testing is still required before live entry.
