Ways to learn

From a first try to serious development.

Choose group workshops, focused one-to-one tuition or the regular after-school programme.

Workshops

Hands-on group sessions in recording, vocals, production, DJ skills, photography, graffiti, breakdancing, skating and creative design. Ideal for schools, youth groups and young people who want to try something new.

One-to-one tuition

Personal sessions built around the learner: studio recording, production, mixing, DJ skills, photography, skate coaching, art and other Hub disciplines. Progress at your own pace with direct guidance.

After-school clubs

Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday groups for different ages. Young people rotate through music, art and sport, make friends and discover where their strongest interests and abilities are.

The goal

Confidence, creativity and real-world skills. Hub Academy is designed to reward effort and progression rather than simply attendance—giving children visible milestones they can be proud of.

Achievement system

Learn it. Earn it. Wear it.

Members receive a Hub Academy T-shirt and earn scout-style patches as they develop skills, complete challenges and support others.
