SoundBunker Algarve

Record Label.

A home for artist development, independent releases and music made inside SoundBunker. We work with local and emerging artists to build better records, stronger identities and sustainable catalogues — taking music from the first idea through production, recording, mixing, mastering and release.

Develop · Produce · Release

Giving artists a route forward.

SoundBunker is built around working artists. The label extends what happens in the studio: finding talent, developing songs, creating professional masters and helping artists get their music heard. Our aim is to push Algarve artists beyond a one-off studio session and help them create work they can genuinely build on.

Artists can come to us with a finished record, a demo or simply an idea. We can help shape the production, record vocals, mix and master the track, prepare the release and develop the next steps around the artist.

Founder · Producer · Artist

Lofty.

Lofty is the artist and producer project of SoundBunker founder Steve Liddle, bringing more than 25 years of recording, production, mixing and mastering experience into an active release project. His catalogue crosses house, electronic and dance music, alongside official remix work and collaborations. That working-artist perspective is central to the label: SoundBunker develops artists from inside a real, active music-making environment.
